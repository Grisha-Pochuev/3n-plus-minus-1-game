# Article version archive

This directory preserves the successive article drafts and their accompanying
files. The contents of `v1` through `v6` are historical snapshots copied
without mathematical revision. They are retained for provenance and may
contain proof gaps documented in their audit or problem files; they must not be
cited as valid proofs of the unconditional no-`DRAW` theorem.

`v7/` contains the complete attached version 7.0 package: manuscript source,
PDF, audit, verification script and output, PDF preflight reports, README, and
checksums.

`v8/` contains the audit-ready article produced on 12 August 2026, together
with its hostile-audit report, README, and checksums. Version 8 is
self-contained for its stated results. Its scope is a rigorous reduction with
an explicit open refinement obligation, not a complete proof of the game.

The canonical working source remains `../audit_ready_reduction.tex`, and the
canonical reviewed PDF remains `../../output/pdf/audit_ready_reduction.pdf`.
The version 8 manuscript and PDF are exact byte-for-byte snapshots of those
canonical files.
